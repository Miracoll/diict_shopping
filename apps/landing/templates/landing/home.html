What's inside

The pieces already wired up and running.

Verified accounts

Registration sends a verification email before an account goes live, with password reset and change flows built alongside it.

🛒

Cart that persists

Every shopper gets one cart tied to their account. Add, update and remove items — subtotals and totals are computed server side.

📦

Orders end to end

Check out into a real order and follow it through pending, paid, processing, shipped and delivered.

Built for the app
